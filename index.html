Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and investing in real estate

You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score (typically 620+), a down payment saved (usually 10-20% of the home price), and you plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years. Our team can help assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage pre-approval services.

Spring and early summer (March through June) are traditionally the best times to sell, as buyers are more active and homes show better with good weather. However, market conditions, local factors, and your personal timeline are more important than the season. We can help you determine the optimal timing based on current market conditions in your area.

Your offer should be based on comparable recent sales (comps), the property's condition, how long it has been on the market, and current local competition. Our agents prepare a detailed comparative market analysis before you submit an offer.

Closing costs include lender fees, appraisal fees, title insurance, and taxes, typically ranging from 2% to 5% of the purchase price. Both buyers and sellers have their own set of closing costs, though seller concessions can sometimes be negotiated.

Yes, a professional home inspection is strongly recommended. It uncovers hidden structural, electrical, or plumbing issues and gives you leverage to negotiate repairs or price adjustments before closing.

Once an offer is accepted, the closing process typically takes 30 to 45 days for financed purchases, and as little as 10 to 14 days for all-cash transactions.
